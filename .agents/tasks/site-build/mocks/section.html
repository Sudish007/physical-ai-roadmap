12.1 Purchase rule link

JUDGMENT, high confidence Buy hardware only after the simulator exposes a hardware-only unknown: sensor bias, timing, rolling shutter, multipath, slip, backlash, compliance, contact; actuator saturation, motor variation, battery sag, thermal throttling, radio loss, deadline misses; calibration repeatability, connector failures, operator error, emergency-stop procedure; or a sim-to-real claim that a second simulator, held-out scene, SIL, or HIL cannot answer. Never buy hardware to learn kinematics, PID/LQR, ROS topics, planning, elementary RL, mission logic, or experiment design.

12.3 Sim-to-hardware release criteria link

All applicable gates before powered motion: (1) frozen test suite with no tuning on final seeds; (2) independent safety monitor on speed, position, attitude, force, and command age with tested hold/stop/land; (3) measured p99 latency below half the control period including sensing, transport, inference, and actuation; (4) measured hardware parameter ranges inside the simulation envelope; (5) under IID Bernoulli trials from the frozen simulated test distribution, at least 300 zero-violation trials before even a one-sided 95% bound near 1%, using the exact limit 1 − 0.05^(1/N) (approximately 3/N; Hanley & Lippman-Hand, JAMA 1983doi.org; NIST e-Handbook §7.2.4.1itl.nist.gov); correlated trials, shared-mode failures, and incomplete scenario coverage prevent a broader claim, and this is evidence, not certification; (6) independent logs of commanded and measured state, watchdog, interventions, battery and thermal state, synchronized video; (7) physical progression power-off → actuators disconnected or propellers off → restrained low-energy motion → guarded or caged test → approved envelope; (8) trained second person, exclusion zone, kill switch, fire plan, property permission, current legal and airspace review; (9) stop on unexpected arming, stale-command motion, guard breach, flyaway tendency, battery damage, repeated estimator divergence, or unexplained timing overrun.

12.6 India procurement execution gate link

Before any order, create a one-page purchase record with: exact manufacturer part/SKU and radio module; two written quotes where possible, including one authorized Indian channel; stock date; GST invoice status; shipping, battery restrictions, customs classification/duty/brokerage; warranty/returns; DGFT written position for any imported UAS; DoT/WPC equipment-authorization, licence and import position for the exact transmitter; mandatory containment, independent stop, charging/fire controls, calibration, wear parts and two failure spares; host-compute compatibility; and the Rung-6 result that the purchase will test. Any unresolved mandatory field means do not order. A marketplace listing, foreign CE/FCC mark, or payment acceptance is not clearance.
