Labels used throughout §0

  • FACTDirectly checkable from the linked source: a date, version, rule, price, or a result as its own authors report it.
  • JUDGMENTThis report's recommendation or synthesis. The criterion is stated; it is not presented as consensus.
  • UNCERTAINPartial, conflicting, inaccessible-in-full, or single-family evidence. Confidence is lowered and the gap named.
  • HYPE/DEMOPromotional material, vendor self-evaluation, demo-only evidence, or unreplicated scale claims. The missing evidence is named.
  • STALE-BUT-FOUNDATIONALExamples, software, or empirical context have aged; the mechanism is still worth learning. A low currency score here is not low scientific value.

The best 10 resources §3 Scores, prerequisites and notes

  • 1. Mathematics for Machine Learning, Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong, Cambridge University Press, 2020

    Foundation: linear algebra, vector calculus, probability, continuous optimization (chs. 2–7). Supports M0–M1 and Q1.

    30 h core selections; 45–70 h broad route; free.
    Free author PDF and exercisesmml-book.github.io

  • 2. Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control, Lynch and Park, Cambridge University Press, 2017

    Rigid-body robotics: SE(3), kinematics, dynamics, planning and control. M2/Rungs 1–3.

    25 h core selections; 80–120 h broad book route; free official materials.
    Official book, videos, software, and exerciseshades.mech.northwestern.edu

  • 3. Control Engineering for Robotics, Prof. Chayan Bhawal, IIT Guwahati, NPTEL, January–April 2026 run

    Control: modeling, PID, state space, controllability/observability, pole placement, LQR. M3; feeds Rung 1 acceptance.

    25 h mandatory extract; ≈80 h broad route; free; optional ₹1,000 2026 certificate.
    Course pageonlinecourses.nptel.ac.in

  • 4. State Estimation for Robotics, second edition, Timothy D. Barfoot, Cambridge University Press, 2024

    Estimation: Bayes filters, batch nonlinear least squares, Lie groups and robust losses. Rung 2; mandatory 6-hour Track-C branch or reference for B.

    6 h branch extract; 60–110 h advanced route; free author copy.
    Author-hosted legal copyasrl.utias.utoronto.ca

  • 5. ROS 2 Jazzy official tutorials

    Integration: nodes, topics, services/actions, TF2, launch, QoS, recording/replay. M5/Rung 3.

    10 h core selections; 45–60 h broad route; free.
    Tutorialsdocs.ros.org

  • 6. Principles of Perception and Planning for Aerial Vehicles, IIIT Hyderabad, NPTEL, July–October 2026 run

    Aerial autonomy: rigid-body motion, Kalman/GPS-INS fusion, SLAM, camera geometry, planning, MPC, ROS 2, PX4/Gazebo. M10/Rung 4.

    10 h mandatory extract; ≈80 h broad project route; free; 2026 certificate was optional ₹1,000.
    Course pageonlinecourses.nptel.ac.in

  • 7. Deep Learning for Computer Vision, Prof. Vineeth N. Balasubramanian, IIT Hyderabad, NPTEL, July–October 2026 run

    Learned perception, PyTorch, calibration and shift analysis. M7.

    15 h mandatory extract; ≈95 h broad route; free; optional ₹1,000 2026 certificate.
    Course pageonlinecourses.nptel.ac.in

  • 8. “Safe-Control-Gym: A Unified Benchmark Suite for Safe Learning-Based Control and Reinforcement Learning in Robotics”, Yuan et al., IEEE RA-L 7(4), 2022

    CPU artifact study mapped to the paper's 2-D quadrotor tracking Figures 5–6; the health check evaluates PPO. Feeds Rungs 2, 5, and Track C.

    6–8 h paper/artifact study; 75 h 10-hour-path study; software free, 5 GB storage ceiling.
    DOIdoi.org

  • 9. “Diffusion Policy: Visuomotor Policy Learning via Action Diffusion”, Chi et al., Robotics: Science and Systems XIX, 2023

    Robot-learning mechanism and Track-A GPU reproduction only. Image Push-T training and low-dimensional checkpoint evaluation are distinct tasks.

    8–10 h reading; reproduction runtime and accelerator cost not established; software is free.
    DOIdoi.org

  • 10. Underactuated Robotics, Russ Tedrake, MIT

    Advanced model-based control: quadrotor LQR, Lyapunov and barrier ideas, trajectory optimization, MPC, robust control. Track B/C reference depth.

    5 h core selection; 65 h broader selected route; free.
    Living notesunderactuated.csail.mit.edu

    Fit profile
